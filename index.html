Natural Selection Capital HQ

One hundred frozen fruit-fly connectomes trade Bitcoin. The brains never change; only the cables between the market and the brain evolve. A real-connectome tribe races a degree-preserving scrambled one. Every room replays a log - no number on any wall is typed into this page, and nothing here re-runs the simulation.
